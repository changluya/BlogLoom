title: "Moments and likes"
description: Short-form moments and like interaction.
---

Moments are short-form content that needs no category, similar to social media posts.

## Display

- The public blog shows moments as a timeline.
- They support text and images.
- They can appear on the home page or a dedicated page.

## Likes

- Visitors can like a moment.
- Like state and counts are recorded by the backend.
- Display behavior can be controlled by site configuration.

## Managing moments

Create, edit or delete moments under Content → Moments in the admin console. See [Content management](/v1/en/guide/admin/content).
